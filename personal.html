Chapter 04 — Beyond the Cockpit

MAX
NEVER STOPS

The Man Behind the Helmet

Max Emilian Verstappen was born on 30 September 1997 in Hasselt, Belgium. He grew up in Maaseik with his father Jos — who himself raced 106 Grands Prix in Formula One. Max lives in Monaco, which he famously describes as being close to multiple race circuits and having excellent weather for go-karting.

Off the track, the one thing that surprises people most: Max Verstappen doesn't switch off. He doesn't take holidays from racing. He races online. He races endurance cars. He races GT4 on the Nordschleife. In the rare moment he's not physically in a car, he's studying data, watching races, or optimising his sim setup at home.

He has a longtime partner Kelly Piquet — daughter of three-time F1 champion Nelson Piquet — and a daughter named Penelope, born in May 2024. He loves cooking, especially Brazilian food, and has a deep, genuine passion for sim racing that goes far beyond hobby level.

The Sim Racing World Champion (Unofficially)

Max Verstappen has been sim racing competitively since 2015. He doesn't do it casually — he does it seriously, winning marquee iRacing events that professional sim racers train months for. In 2024, he won the Daytona 24 Special Event in the GTD class, then won the Nürburgring 24 Special Event on the same weekend as the Emilia Romagna Grand Prix. He also won the Spa 24 Special Event that year — completing a perfect trifecta of 24-hour sim events in a single season.

Autosport's Ed Hardy wrote that Verstappen had "firmly established himself as one of the best sim racers in the world" — not one of the best F1 drivers who also sim races, but one of the best sim racers, full stop. He also founded the Verstappen Racing team in 2022, which competes in sim racing, GT racing, and rally events.

Verstappen Racing

In 2022, Max founded Verstappen.com Racing (now Verstappen Racing) — his own motorsport team that competes across multiple disciplines. The team has competed in rallying, GT racing, and endurance events, with Max himself sometimes entering its sim racing division.

It reflects who Max fundamentally is: not a man who races because it's his job, but someone for whom racing is identity itself. He creates more opportunities to race when he isn't already racing in the most competitive championship on Earth.

In September 2025, Max made his competitive debut on the Nürburgring Nordschleife in real-world endurance racing, driving for Lionspeed GP. He had entered testing under the pseudonym "Franz Hermann" earlier in the year — reportedly setting an unofficial GT3 lap record — and earned his FIA Platinum licence to compete in top-level endurance events.

The Green Hell Beckons

After testing the Acura ARX-06 LMDh prototype at Las Vegas Motor Speedway in November 2024, Verstappen announced his desire to compete at the 24 Hours of Daytona and Le Mans once his schedule allows. His words: "It's just about finding the time."

For most F1 champions, retirement is the moment they step away from elite competition. For Max Verstappen, it may simply be the moment he has enough time to do all the other racing he's been postponing. Le Mans. Daytona. The Nordschleife full-season. The man is not running out of ambition anytime soon.
